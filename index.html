Money savers and tips

Amazon Baby Registry, kept private

Make a registry, set it to private, and add these items. With Prime you get a free welcome box (add 10 items, $10 bought from it) and a 15% completion discount (up to $300) starting 60 days before the due date. Buying is not required to make one.

Breast pump through insurance

Most plans must cover a pump. A service like Aeroflow checks coverage and ships one free. Start in December.

Free car seat check

Safe Kids Maricopa County runs free checks. Install the seat by week 36 and get it checked.

Don’t overbuy newborn sizes

Many babies outgrow newborn clothes and diapers in 2–4 weeks. Buy one pack of newborn diapers and lean on size 1 and 0–3 months.

Hospital freebies

Most hospitals send you home with diapers, wipes, peri bottles, pads, and mesh underwear. Take them all.

Buy new, not used

Car seats and crib mattresses should be new. Most other things (bouncer, tub, clothes) are fine secondhand.

February in Mesa

Nights dip into the 40s. Use a sleep sack, not loose blankets, and thin layers in the car seat, never a puffy coat under the straps.

Safe sleep

Baby sleeps on their back, alone, on a firm flat surface with only a fitted sheet. No bumpers, pillows, or loose blankets.
